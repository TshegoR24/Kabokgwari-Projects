02 Line — The Studio

One line runs from
first sketch to final coat.

Every project we take on is designed and built by the same team, so the idea that gets approved in the first meeting is the one that gets handed over on completion day.

"We don't hand a concept to someone else to build. The person who draws the room is accountable for how it feels once you're standing in it."

Kabokgwari Projects works across residential and commercial interiors — from a single room refresh to a full renovation. We plan the space, source the materials, manage the build, and style the final result, so you deal with one studio from concept to handover.

Founded in 2017 by Malatji Douglass and Nelly Tsatsanki Malatji, the studio has grown from its first projects into a full design-and-build service across Limpopo — still run by the same two people who started it.

03 Form — What We Do

Four services, one build.

Take one, or all four in sequence. Most full renovations move through every stage below.

— 01

Interior Design & Space Planning

Layout, flow, and a full concept board before a single wall moves — so you can see the room before it's built.

— 02

Renovations & Fit-Outs

Structural changes, finishes and fixtures managed on site, on a schedule you can see the whole way through.

— 03

Décor, Styling & Furnishing

Furniture, textiles, art and lighting sourced and placed — the layer that turns a finished space into a home.

— 04

Maintenance & Handover Care

A seasonal check-in after we leave — vents, fixtures and finishes kept in the condition they were handed over in.

04 Light — How We Work

Four stages, in daylight.

No stage starts until you've signed off on the one before it — so there are no surprises when the invoice arrives.

i.

Consult

A walkthrough of the space, a conversation about budget and how you'll use each room.

ii.

Concept

Layout, materials and a mood board, priced and ready to approve before anything is ordered.

iii.

Build

On-site work managed against a fixed schedule, with regular photo updates as the space takes shape.

iv.

Handover

A final walkthrough, styling touches placed, and a maintenance note for the months ahead.
